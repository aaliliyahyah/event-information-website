A space for ideas.
A place to begin.

SET Days brings the School of Engineering and Technology community together at NU Fairview on December 2–4, 2026. This website imagines a celebration of the people, projects, and possibilities behind engineering and technology.

The proposed program makes room for both first attempts and fresh perspectives. Through hands-on workshops, friendly challenges, and student exhibits, participants can turn classroom concepts into something they can share.

Engineering and technology students are the intended participants, with other NU Fairview students welcome to explore the exhibits. Whether you enjoy coding, building, or asking questions, there is a place for your curiosity.

Find your spark.

Six proposed activities. Plenty of ways to get involved.

  • COMMUNITY

    Opening Program

    Meet fellow students and discover the ideas shaping this three-day celebration.

  • DISCOVERY

    Innovation Exhibit

    Explore student prototypes, hear their stories, and ask the makers how they work.

  • HANDS-ON

    Web Development Workshop

    Practice the basics of HTML and create a small page of your own. Bring a laptop.

  • CHALLENGE

    Robotics Challenge

    Work with a team to guide a robot through a friendly obstacle-course challenge.

  • CONVERSATION

    Tech Career Talk

    Explore possible career paths and discuss the skills students can start developing.

  • SHOW & TELL

    Project Showcase

    Share a class project, explain your process, and exchange constructive feedback.

How to join an activity

  1. Choose an activity from the program.
  2. Check its proposed time and venue.
  3. Follow the participation steps before joining.

A few terms to know

HTML
The markup language used to structure content on a web page.
Robotics
The design, construction, and programming of robots.
Prototype
An early model used to explore and test an idea.

  2. Ask the organizing committee about availability and any team requirements.
