A space for ideas.
A place to begin.

SET Days brings the School of Engineering and Technology community together at NU Fairview on December 2–4, 2026. It is a three-day celebration of the people, projects, and possibilities behind engineering and technology.

The program makes room for both first attempts and fresh perspectives. Through hands-on workshops, friendly challenges, and student exhibits, participants can turn classroom concepts into something they can share.

Engineering and technology students are invited to participate, and all NU Fairview students are welcome to explore the exhibits. Whether you enjoy coding, building, or asking questions, there is a place for your curiosity.

Find your spark.

Six featured activities. Plenty of ways to get involved.

  • COMMUNITY

    Opening Program

    Meet fellow students and discover the ideas shaping this three-day celebration.

  • DISCOVERY

    Innovation Exhibit

    Explore student prototypes, hear their stories, and ask the makers how they work.

  • HANDS-ON

    Web Development Workshop

    Practice the basics of HTML and create a small page of your own. Bring a laptop.

  • CHALLENGE

    Robotics Challenge

    Work with a team to guide a robot through a friendly obstacle-course challenge.

  • CONVERSATION

    Tech Career Talk

    Explore possible career paths and discuss the skills students can start developing.

  • SHOW & TELL

    Project Showcase

    Share a class project, explain your process, and exchange constructive feedback.

How to join an activity

  1. Choose an activity from the program.
  2. Check its scheduled time and venue.
  3. Follow the participation steps before joining.

The design, construction, and programming of robots.
Prototype
